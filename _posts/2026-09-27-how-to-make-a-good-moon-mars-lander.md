# How To Make A Good Moon/Mars Lander
So you’re a space company who wants to beat Elon Musk to going to the Moon or Mars. So if you wanna land you better make a GOOD lander.


## Rough Design:
So, to actually make the lander you will need money (imagining you have net worth of Elon Musk)  you can make a lander. The first step is to not make your lander tall and skinny. If it is, there is less stable points on the lander or they are worse at keeping it stable, like the landing legs. If you made this mistake, try to make it wider and shorter.
Another mistake you should not make is underestimating gravity. The moon’s gravity pulls about 1.625m/s and Mars’s is 3.71m/s. You have to be careful

## Interior View
This is also important if your crew don’t want to die. First is NEVER to make your living space square. Sure you might say “bUt HOuSes ArE SQuArE” , That is because theres ventilation. If you make it square on the interior (including the exterior) it will crack at the corners leading to air leaks.

## Prepare For Mars 
Mars is Worse than Luna. Mars has barely no solar power so you need to be really energy efficient, And it also has an atmosphere which is 100x thinner than Earth’s. You can still slow down with the atmosphere but it is real hard. It has the same requirements as the Moon, Only you need a heat shield and a parachute.
