## Problems with returning
There are problems with returning though and you do not want to end up like Mark Watney on mars for 1000 days. One problem is that you will probably have not enough fuel or too little thrust to go back. Another problem is that you can “fall down” to Mars if your orbit is staying there a little long because of orbital decay. That means the energy that got you there is now gone and you’re falling to death. There are billion problems that can happen like your lander crashes because you’re dumb enough to use parachutes.
