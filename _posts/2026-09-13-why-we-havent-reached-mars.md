The announcement of Artemis Promised to reach Mars in several years. There is one problem. Funding, And thats not even the MAIN problem.

1. Funding

Since 1969 during the moon landing funding has been increased, but now  its decreasing. Because of the US-(we dont talk about it) war. Since the war needs resources which is money, so funding for NASA is decreased.

2. Fuel/Power

This is the moon but 100x harder. We have sent robotic probes but it is hard, you'd have to wait for the EXACT time to go. A 1 day delay is delayed by a year. Also it takes A LOT of fuel to go there. Most of the things are one-way but for a 2 way (Earth to Mars to Earth) you'd need 21200km/h of Delta-V! And that is not a big deal for you but a big deal for rockets.

3. Crew

Even Though the ISS has been staying for 10 years doesnt mean that we can reach Mars, Since Solar Energy is 43% of it used to be on Earth. RTG's are better but there is a massive Plutonium shortage. So solar power, The crew also would need tons of resources. They would need the Oxygenator on the ISS, a hydrophonics farm, and a constant water supply. For Any amount of crew.

So it is possible to reach Mars but barely, And theres no chance that they'd have money.
