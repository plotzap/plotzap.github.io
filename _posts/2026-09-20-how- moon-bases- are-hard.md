You know how NASA is making a moon base on 2030 or something close? Well that might not be possible.

1. Cost:

NASA's funding is low, and the cost of rockets are high especially for big ones.

2. Deadliness:

Since the moon is tidally locked one side is hell and one is deadly frigid. Also the moons orbit is roughly a month so its 15 days of death and another 15 days of death. Luckily the ISS has this solved so no biggie.

3. The Probability:

I mean its the probability of failure. Anything could Stop any success. A leak? Death. Few days off? More days off.

Conclusion: Possible but unlikely.
